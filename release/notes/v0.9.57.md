# 5tratMux 0.9.57

Local AI guidance now accepts exact whole percentages and a named remainder.
For example, `BCH is the goal; maintain 10% on DGB; put the rest towards BCH`
produces a checked 90% BCH / 10% DGB plan for compatible selected miners.
The operator supplies the percentages; the app calculates and validates them,
while the local model interprets other preferences and reviews the proposed plan.

Conflicting instructions explain the conflict and keep current routing. Naming
BCH as the primary goal while sending 10% to DGB and the other 90% to BC2 would
leave BCH at 0%, so the instruction needs a BCH share or a different remainder.
Missing or unhealthy routes, incompatible algorithms, unsupported fractions or
ranges, and saved reserve conflicts also hold routing.

This update retains the miner login compatibility and concurrent worker-state
persistence fixes tested on the development node, enriches block wins when pool
worker evidence arrives later, and reduces repeated notification acknowledgment
failures using persistent retry backoff without discarding pending records.

The release uses signed manifests and protected native images for amd64 and
arm64. Installed mission settings, worker allocations and licence state are
preserved by the updater.
