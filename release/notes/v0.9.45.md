5tratMux 0.9.45 makes external-pool removal reliable and preserves operator edits while background work is running.

- Removing an external pool now permanently removes its saved profile, credentials and routing references, including when it is the last or most recently edited pool.
- Miners using the removed pool reconnect onto a compatible remaining route. Unrelated miners stay connected. Saved AI restore plans cannot bring the deleted pool back.
- Removal displays progress, prevents duplicate clicks and survives navigation away from the page. Success appears only after removal is verified.
- Background pool research can no longer reactivate a disabled pool or overwrite newer account and connection settings. Research for a changed destination is discarded.
- Stratum SSL URLs retain TLS through saving, editing, probing and mining connections. Certificates are verified, with no plaintext fallback.
- Background hardware telemetry can no longer undo a miner rename, including repeated edits within the same second.

Configuration, installation identity, licence state and saved mining routes are preserved during update. Existing SHA-256 and Scrypt routing improvements from 0.9.44 are included.

Validation: all 487 Python tests and the JavaScript behavioral checks passed, including deletion races, affected socket disconnection, TLS certificate validation and concurrent edits. Native AMD64 and ARM64 packages passed architecture, protected-runtime and startup checks. Live testing confirmed removal survives immediate full-page navigation, saved credentials are gone, unrelated miners remain connected and mining resumes after update. The main app screens loaded without browser errors.
