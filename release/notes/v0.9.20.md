5tratMux v0.9.20 is a full control-surface and live-monitoring rebuild.

The app is now organised around five clear workspaces: Live, Route, Automate,
Setup and System. Desktop, kiosk, tablet and phone layouts have been rebuilt
independently so controls, cards and evidence remain readable without page
zooming or horizontal overflow. A dedicated phone navigation mode and a
full-screen monitor with adjustable text are included.

The Live workspace keeps the miner-to-MUX-to-chain animation, but now joins it
to authoritative route evidence. It shows current delivered hash, warm routes,
active destinations, MUX state, requested and awarded fleet share, fallback
reserve, the leading 5tratPack and model, and exact restoration state. The MUX
centre is informational, while explicit buttons control the monitor, evidence
history and manual MUX actions.

When a MUX response is armed, the monitor now explains why. Trigger-specific
graphs cover verified difficulty cliffs, published-value and expected-return
dislocations, and abnormal block-cadence or catch-up windows. The graph,
allocation, decision source, leading pack/model and saved-route restoration are
kept together on the monitoring screen.

5tratPacks are now operational controls rather than a future-work list. The
included bounded models cover Bayesian route confidence, bandit selection,
change-point and regime detection, freshness and propagation quality,
simulation, portfolio allocation, delivery confidence, exposure balancing and
bias diagnostics. They rank relative route evidence and may adjust a detector's
bounded allocation. Existing deterministic evidence, safety gates, fallback
reserve and restoration remain authoritative. 5tratPacks are off by default,
require Luna or better when enabled, and retain user-controlled cadence, call
and spend limits.

The release also improves fleet recovery reporting, mobile disclosures,
responsive cards, block-route provenance, live evidence history, signed-update
verification and static-asset packaging. Existing miners, routes, pool details,
AI settings, API credentials, licensing state and MUX-to-app live hash
injection are preserved.

The mathematical scores are comparative routing signals. They do not claim to
predict or guarantee a block win.
