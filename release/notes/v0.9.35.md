5tratMux v0.9.35 strengthens warm-route recovery for Braiins OS, LuxOS,
Antminer, Koi, AxeOS and other Stratum V1 miners.

AI allocation changes now reconcile new compatible pool lanes into the
existing warm proxy session. A route-set change no longer closes a healthy
miner connection merely because its saved profile predates the live routing
engine state.

If an upstream lane fails during a share submission, 5tratMux returns one
clean rejected-share response to the miner, keeps the miner TCP connection
open, and retries the affected lane. Pending submissions are bounded by a
watchdog so a silent upstream cannot leave the miner waiting indefinitely.
Late duplicate responses are suppressed safely.

Automatic proxy difficulty is now hash-rate and model aware. High-power
miners begin with a stronger common share target, and live sessions may move
upward as telemetry becomes available. The proxy never advertises a target
below the strictest active upstream lane and never lowers difficulty inside a
live session. Every resulting share remains valid for the selected node, and
block-valid solutions are forwarded normally. Manual difficulty limits remain
unchanged.

The full 444-test suite passed. The AMD64 image was then tested on the live
.235 node with all seven miners connected. A bounded MUX allocation and
restore completed without dropping a miner, and a warm AxeXEC backend outage
and recovery preserved all seven downstream sessions with no proxy error.
The ARM64 image was compiled natively and verified to expose both dashboard
and Stratum listeners.

Local settings, licence state, miner identities, wallets and routing state are
preserved by the updater.
