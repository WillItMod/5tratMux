# 5tratMux 0.9.52 — Licence backup and PC replacement

Paid users can restore their saved licence-proof JSON after reinstalling
5tratumOS or moving to a replacement PC, without requesting a manual rebind
key. Open **System → Licensing**, upload the proof and confirm the move.

- Each paid licence includes five self-service moves in total. The licence's
  original annual expiry or lifetime plan is preserved, not restarted.
- Activation of the replacement and retirement of the old binding are one
  authority transaction. Duplicate retries on the current installation do
  not use an additional move.
- Remaining moves are shown in the licence screen. The current proof is
  saved privately on the installation and can be downloaded again after a
  browser refresh. Older valid backups remain usable within the same limit.
- Existing manual support rebinds remain available for recovery cases.
- Online old installations lose paid access at their next successful
  licensing check. Offline installations retain only their existing signed
  lease/grace; an offline device cannot be disabled instantly.

Internet access is required to restore. Keep the proof private because it
contains the licence recovery secret. A missing proof or exhausted allowance
requires support; an expired licence is not renewed by moving it.

Includes all 0.9.51 Local AI and MUX reliability fixes, with signed native
AMD64 and ARM64 runtime archives and minified browser assets. The private
application and licensing authority source are not included in these public
releases. Existing application settings and installation identity are retained
when installing this update; upgrading itself does not consume a move.
