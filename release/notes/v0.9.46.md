5tratMux 0.9.46 fixes Scrypt connection failures, including Nexus L1 miners, and restores visibility of installed Scrypt pools.

- Recognises Nexus L1 and additional verified Scrypt models, and preserves a saved Scrypt algorithm when firmware reports a generic client name.
- Lets Scrypt pools set their actual share difficulty. Mux no longer injects unsupported difficulty commands or waits for PowPow to lower its normal starting difficulty.
- Preserves the subscription and coinbase when a miner waits for subscribe before authorising, including on a single pool without extranonce-switching support.
- Corrects Scrypt proof-derived hashrate and cadence estimates.
- Shows PowPow and AxeDGB Scrypt in Pools as app-managed difficulty routes. Reads and preserves PowPow's configured listener port and real serving status, including Litecoin-only bootstrap during Dogecoin sync.

Includes all 0.9.45 external-pool removal, verified TLS, and operator-edit preservation fixes. Local configuration, licence identity, wallets and saved allocations are preserved.

Validation: all 518 Python tests and three JavaScript behavioral suites passed, including native Python 3.11 and local TCP Nexus handshake/share-translation fixtures. AMD64 and ARM64 MAIN and DEV packages passed native startup, architecture, checksum and protected-runtime checks. Physical Nexus L1 hardware has not been tested; the TCP fixture acknowledges simulated submissions without validating proof of work. Current PowPow already contains the optional-command compatibility fix and needs no companion update for this release.
