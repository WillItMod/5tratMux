5tratMux v0.9.33 improves Stratum V1 compatibility for high-power SHA256
miners running Braiins OS, BOSminer, stock Antminer firmware, LuxOS, Luxminer,
bmminer, and related S19/S21/T19/T21/X19 families.

These clients often identify themselves by firmware or model during the
subscribe handshake rather than by an algorithm-specific worker prefix.
5tratMux now classifies those identities as SHA256 without weakening the
existing Scrypt separation, allowing them to authorize, receive difficulty
and live jobs, and remain connected to their warm routed pool set.

The update was exercised on the AMD64 test node with Braiins-style configure,
subscribe and authorize messages. The session remained open, received live
work before and after a container restart, and the node's seven existing
miners reattached successfully. AMD64 and ARM64 release images have both
passed the signed-release verification checks.

Local settings, licence state, miner identities, wallets, and routing state
are preserved by the updater.
