5tratMux v0.9.34 completes the Braiins OS and Antminer recovery work started
in v0.9.33.

The proxy now keeps the miner-side Stratum connection open when an active
warm pool lane restarts or briefly disappears. It holds the downstream
session, retries the affected upstream lanes, and sends a clean compatible
job as soon as a fully negotiated lane is ready. This prevents Braiins OS,
AxeOS and similar firmware from entering their multi-minute dead-pool or
suspended backoff merely because a pool service restarted.

Recovery is declared only after the upstream has completed configure,
subscribe, difficulty and job negotiation. Retry state is no longer cleared
by a TCP connection alone, and startup failures can select another ready lane
without closing the miner socket.

The AMD64 candidate was tested on the live .235 node by deliberately stopping
both active SHA256 pool backends. All seven miners stayed connected throughout
the outage, entered held or retrying state, and returned to healthy routing
without a proxy error. A separate Braiins-style configure, subscribe,
authorize and live-job session also completed and remained open. AMD64 and
ARM64 images passed the signed-release verification checks.

Local settings, licence state, miner identities, wallets, and routing state
are preserved by the updater.
