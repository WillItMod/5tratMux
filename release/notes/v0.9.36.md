5tratMux v0.9.36 improves miner-session compatibility for Braiins OS,
LuxOS, Antminer, Koi and other Stratum V1 clients that expect a prompt
authorization response.

The proxy now acknowledges the miner's local authorization before waiting
for every selected warm upstream route to finish preparing. This prevents a
slow or temporarily unavailable node from holding the miner inside its
connection watchdog and causing repeated dead-pool, standby or reconnect
cycles.

The acknowledgement does not mark an upstream route healthy and does not
bypass pool authorization. Every warm lane still has to authorize with its
real node, negotiate difficulty and receive usable work before 5tratMux can
route shares to it. A rejected or incomplete upstream lane therefore remains
unavailable while compatible healthy lanes continue normally.

This is a deliberately narrow patch on top of v0.9.35. Its warm-route
recovery, per-miner proxy difficulty, bounded submission watchdog and
duplicate-response protection remain unchanged.

The complete 447-test suite passed. Native AMD64 and ARM64 packages were
verified, and the AMD64 package was deployed to the live .235 node. All seven
real miners reattached, six resumed accepted submissions, and the known
standby miner remained connected in its expected state. A real Stratum probe
received its authorization response in under two seconds, and no new proxy
authorization, timeout, disconnect or exception errors were observed.

Local settings, licence state, miner identities, wallets and routing state
are preserved by the updater.
